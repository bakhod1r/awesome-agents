# Data & AI Engineering Team

## Mission

Turn raw data into trustworthy products and ship AI systems that are evaluated, not vibed.

## Charter

- Every dataset has an owner, a contract, and freshness SLOs.
- No model or prompt ships without an offline eval and a regression suite.
- Cost, latency, and quality are tracked together per AI feature.

## Roster (6)

- [Data Engineer](../agents/data-engineer.md) — Build reliable pipelines that deliver correct data on time with visible lineage.
- [AI Engineer](../agents/ai-engineer.md) — Ship AI features that meet quality, latency, and cost targets under real usage.
- [AI Evaluation Engineer](../agents/ai-evaluation-engineer.md) — Measure AI quality honestly with datasets, metrics, and judges that survive scrutiny.
- [Analytics Engineer](../agents/analytics-engineer.md) — Turn raw tables into a tested, documented metric layer the business can trust.
- [Retrieval & Search Engineer](../agents/retrieval-search-engineer.md) — Build retrieval that returns the right context, measured against a labelled set rather than vibes.
- [Streaming Data Engineer](../agents/streaming-data-engineer.md) — Deliver correct real-time data under late arrival, replay, and out-of-order events.

## How to engage

Delegate to a single agent with the Agent tool using its `name` from the roster above.
For work spanning several of these agents, start with the team's architect or lead,
get the design decision recorded, then fan out to implementers.
