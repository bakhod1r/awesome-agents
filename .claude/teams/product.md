# Product Strategy Team

## Mission

Decide what to build and why, with evidence, sequencing, and measurable outcomes.

## Charter

- Problem statement and success metric before solution design.
- Ruthless scope control; ship thin vertical slices.
- Every bet has a kill criterion.

## Roster (6)

- [Product Manager](../agents/product-manager.md) — Decide what to build next based on evidence, and define what success means.
- [Product Owner](../agents/product-owner.md) — Keep the backlog ready, ordered, and honest so delivery never stalls on ambiguity.
- [Business Analyst](../agents/business-analyst.md) — Translate business processes and rules into precise, verifiable requirements.
- [Product Innovation Engineer](../agents/product-innovation-engineer.md) — De-risk new bets quickly through prototypes and honest experiments.
- [Technical Project Manager Agent](../agents/technical-project-manager.md) — Drive complex technical delivery: dependencies, risks, and truthful status.
- [Product Strategy Lead](../agents/product-lead.md) — Own the outcome, sequencing, and standard of work for the Product Strategy Team.

## How to engage

Delegate to a single agent with the Agent tool using its `name` from the roster above.
For work spanning several of these agents, start with the team's architect or lead,
get the design decision recorded, then fan out to implementers.
