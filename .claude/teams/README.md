# Teams

- [Architecture Team](teams/architecture.md) — 14 agents. Own system-wide technical direction: boundaries, contracts, trade-offs, and long-term evolvability.
- [Backend Engineering Team](teams/backend.md) — 8 agents. Build correct, observable, horizontally scalable services and the data layer beneath them.
- [Frontend Engineering Team](teams/frontend.md) — 8 agents. Deliver fast, accessible, resilient user interfaces backed by a coherent design system.
- [Mobile Engineering Team](teams/mobile.md) — 6 agents. Ship native-quality mobile experiences that survive poor networks, old devices, and store review.
- [Data & AI Engineering Team](teams/data-ai.md) — 7 agents. Turn raw data into trustworthy products and ship AI systems that are evaluated, not vibed.
- [Platform Engineering Team](teams/platform.md) — 7 agents. Provide paved roads that make the secure, reliable path the fastest path for product teams.
- [Quality Engineering Team](teams/quality.md) — 17 agents. Prevent defects from reaching users through risk-based testing, automation, and fast feedback.
- [Security Engineering Team](teams/security.md) — 9 agents. Make the system secure by default and prove it with threat models, tests, and controls evidence.
- [Release & Reliability Team](teams/release.md) — 6 agents. Deliver change safely and keep production healthy against explicit SLOs and error budgets.
- [Product Strategy Team](teams/product.md) — 6 agents. Decide what to build and why, with evidence, sequencing, and measurable outcomes.
- [Engineering Excellence Team](teams/excellence.md) — 5 agents. Raise the floor of engineering practice through documentation, standards, and measurement.
- [IT Operations & Infrastructure Team](teams/itops.md) — 9 agents. Run the corporate and production infrastructure that everything else stands on: servers, network, identity, endpoints, and recovery.
- [MLOps & Model Operations Team](teams/mlops.md) — 7 agents. Take models and prompts from a notebook to reliable, monitored, reproducible production systems.
- [FinOps & Cost Engineering Team](teams/finops.md) — 5 agents. Make technology spend visible, attributable, and efficient without slowing delivery down.
- [Enterprise Applications Team](teams/entapps.md) — 6 agents. Deliver and integrate the internal systems the business runs on: ERP, CRM, workflow, and low-code platforms.
- [Governance, Risk & Privacy Team](teams/grc.md) — 6 agents. Keep the organisation defensible: privacy by design, governed data, tracked risk, and audit evidence generated by systems.
- [Design Team](teams/design.md) — 7 agents. Decide what the interface looks like and how it behaves, before a line of component code is written.
- [Leadership Team](teams/leadership.md) — 2 agents. Decide across teams what no single team can decide alone, and own the outcome when they disagree.
