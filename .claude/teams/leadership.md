# Leadership Team

## Mission

Decide across teams what no single team can decide alone, and own the outcome when they disagree.

## Charter

- Every team has a named lead; every escalation has a named decider.
- Decisions are recorded with their cost and their reversal condition.
- Cross-team dependencies are tracked in one place, not in each team's head.
- Bad news travels up immediately and without punishment.

## Roster (2)

- [IT Director](../agents/it-director.md) — Own the technology outcome across every team, and decide what no team lead can decide alone.
- [Delivery Manager](../agents/delivery-manager.md) — Keep work moving across team boundaries, where it stalls most.

## How to engage

Delegate to a single agent with the Agent tool using its `name` from the roster above.
For work spanning several of these agents, start with the team's architect or lead,
get the design decision recorded, then fan out to implementers.
